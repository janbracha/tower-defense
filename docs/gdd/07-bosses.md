# 8. Bosses

**20 bosses**: 2 per Act (a **Warlord** mini-boss on map 3 and an **Act Boss** on map 5). Bosses are mechanics puzzles first and HP sponges second.

## 8.1 Boss Design Rules

1. **Telegraph everything.** Any tower-disabling or Integrity-threatening ability has ≥ 2 s of visual/audio warning.
2. **One signature mechanic per phase.** Phases change the rules, not just the numbers.
3. **Counter-diversity.** Each boss can be beaten with at least three distinct archetypes.
4. **Tenacity.** Hard CC immunity; slows capped at 40% (see combat rules).
5. **Anti-trivialization.** At least two bosses punish each dominant archetype: mono-element, hero-carry, economy-turtle and tower-spam.
6. **Rewards:** guaranteed relic roll, codex lore, Soul Essence bonus, first-clear hero/tower unlocks. Mythic variants add affixes and Mythic-only materials.

Stats given are **relative HP tiers** at Normal: Warlord ≈ 20,000; Act Boss ≈ 60,000 (scaled by difficulty and map wave scalar).

## 8.3 Act I — Verdant Covenant

### 1. Sporemother Myra (Warlord)
- **Lore:** A fungal matriarch who believes she is nursing the forest back to health. She is not wrong, but she is nursing it into herself.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Spore Pods:** every 20 s, 4 pods appear at the path edges. Each pod releases a cloud (−20% attack speed to towers in r1.5) when it bursts after 10 s. Pods can be destroyed early.
  2. **Mycelium Network (Phase 2, 60% HP):** three nodes heal Myra 2%/s each. Destroying nodes stops healing and stuns her for 2 s.
- **Counterplay:** Splash damage on pods; Fire to burn nodes; Hero with mobility.
- **Reward:** Spore Cap relic; unlocks Gloomcap codex entry.

### 2. Old Gnarl, Heart of the Greenwood (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** The oldest tree-mind in Verdancy. He is tired and asks the Guardian for permission to be put to rest, then attacks anyway because the Covenant bound him.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Rootwalls:** every 30 s, rootwalls rise and block the current path, forcing a re-route via the secondary path (unique use of branching paths).
  2. **Heartwood:** after a rootwall is destroyed (it has 3000 HP), a glowing weak point on Gnarl is exposed for 6 s (+100% damage).
  3. **Wildfire hazard:** Fire damage sets sections of the forest aflame: burning tiles damage Gnarl and your towers (use Frost/Cleanse).
- **Counterplay:** Pre-plan covering both routes; Frost to extinguish; heroes for burst during Heartwood.
- **Reward:** Heartwood Seed relic; Unlock Thorn Mossbark's Heroic Mastery path.

## 8.4 Act II — Ashen Sultanate

### 3. Anuket-Zar the Riddlemaster (Warlord)
- **Lore:** A sphinx-queen who has been asking the same riddle for a thousand years. The answer, she says, is "a person who stays."
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Riddle Glyphs:** three glyphs around her show 3 damage types she resists 90% (rotate every 12 s).
  2. **Silent Question (Phase 2):** pauses the battle for 6 s with a quiz: choose a tower school; correct answer (shown subtly in the Codex) grants a 20 s boon, wrong answer applies a minor debuff. Fair and optional.
- **Counterplay:** Rotate damage; keep two schools ready; use Codex knowledge.
- **Reward:** Riddle Shard relic.

### 4. Khet-Ra, the Ashen Sun (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** An emperor who entombed himself to outlast entropy and turned himself into a parody of the sun.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Day Phase (60 s):** Khet-Ra is invulnerable in sunlit tiles; shaded tiles (cast by Shadow towers, Eclipse Pillar, Frost fog, or hero Mirage) allow damage.
  2. **Night Phase (45 s):** vulnerable (+50% damage) but summons mummy hordes from sarcophagi.
  3. **Eclipse Form (30% HP):** merges both: periodic Sun Beams target towers (disable 4 s).
- **Counterplay:** Shadow tools, Frost zone for shade, Radiant to counter mummies.
- **Reward:** Sunscar Brooch relic; unlock Nefari Sunscar mastery.

## 8.5 Act III — Ember Clans

### 5. Warchief Grashka Cinderfang (Warlord)
- **Lore:** The clan's greatest warchief, the first to hear the Heart-Flame's call. She resents being called "possessed".
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Rage Meter:** fills with damage taken. At 100% she charges down the path, trampling blockers and disabling towers in her path.
  2. **Battle Horn:** calls Raiders and Slagthrowers.
  3. **Stagger:** if a Stun/Root breaks her charge (via Thunder Drum or Nature Grove; works as a non-hard "interrupt" on her charge), she is stunned for 4 s and the rage meter resets.
- **Counterplay:** Interrupts; stagger-timed burst; don't over-damage early (she scales on damage).
- **Reward:** Cinderfang War-Paint relic.

### 6. Pyrrax, the Caldera Wyrm (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** The ancient dragon who forged the Cinderhold calderas; she does not hate the Guardian, but her will is no longer her own.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Aerial Phase:** Pyrrax flies, immune to ground-only attacks, drops lava bombs.
  2. **Lava Flood:** every 25 s a lane floods for 10 s: enemies gain +25% speed, towers on the lane are disabled.
  3. **Landing Phase:** after 20% HP loss in the aerial phase she lands to breathe flame cones; wings are weak points.
- **Counterplay:** Anti-air focus during phase 1; Frost to cool lava; Cleansing Light.
- **Reward:** Caldera Scale relic; unlocks Wyrm skin for Kyra.

## 8.6 Act IV — Iron Collective

### 7. Foreman Mk-7 (Warlord)
- **Lore:** A clockwork overseer built to maximize efficiency. He is genuinely distressed by the inefficiency of war.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Assembly Line:** two conveyors on the map spawn Rivet Troopers every 8 s. Destroying a conveyor (4000 HP) stops its stream.
  2. **Hijack:** every 25 s one of your towers is hijacked and fires on its neighbors for 6 s unless cleansed or the tower is sold/re-set (the tower becomes "Tampered").
  3. **Overtime (50% HP):** doubles conveyor spawn rate for 20 s.
- **Counterplay:** Cleanse; destroy conveyors; strong Storm.
- **Reward:** Foreman's Clipboard relic (a very bureaucratic relic).

### 8. Leviathan Mk-IX, the Great Engine (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** The Collective's mobile capital: a city-sized train. The citizens are on board, which makes the Guardian's choices complicated.
- **Mechanics:** The Leviathan has **5 cars**, each with a module that must be destroyed separately:
  - **Shield Car:** projects a dome over the train.
  - **Artillery Car:** outranges towers.
  - **Repair Car:** heals the train.
  - **Drone Car:** releases Repair Drones.
  - **Boiler Car:** explodes (damages everything on the rail) when destroyed.
  Cars can be destroyed in any order. The Engine (head) is only vulnerable after 3 cars fall. **Civilian cars** (optional) can be spared for a star-bonus: destroying them gives a negative narrative outcome but a faster win (choice).
- **Counterplay:** Targeting priority; Planar Lock vs artillery; Storm to drone swarms.
- **Reward:** Engine Core relic; Brannoc Ironvein mastery.

## 8.7 Act V — Frostbound Reach

### 9. Jarl Skadi Rimeheart (Warlord)
- **Lore:** A duelist who has never lost. She respects the hero more than the tower.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Duel Challenge:** at 75% HP, challenges the hero: both enter a sealed arena (towers in radius still fire at normal rate). If no hero was brought, a spectral Duelist is lent to the player for the fight.
  2. **Ice Floor:** freezes a lane; enemies move +30% faster and cannot be blocked.
  3. **Frostbrand:** her sword freezes a tower.
- **Counterplay:** Ensure the hero is healthy; Sanctuary support; strong tower coverage over the arena.
- **Reward:** Rimeheart Blade relic.

### 10. Wintermaw (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** A frozen leviathan sleeping beneath the lake; the Reach feeds him the dead in winter.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Thin Ice:** the lake is built from ice tiles that crack as Wintermaw pounds from below. Cracked tiles become holes (towers on them are lost to the water unless Sold within 3 s: refund 100%).
  2. **Surface:** every 30 s Wintermaw breaches, hitting a line of towers, then becomes vulnerable for 8 s.
  3. **Blizzard Escalation:** range −10% per phase.
- **Counterplay:** Place towers on stable (stone) tiles; Fire to thaw; Gale Perch for blizzard.
- **Reward:** Wintermaw Fang relic.

## 8.8 Act VI — Crystalline Choir

### 11. Cantor Prime (Warlord)
- **Lore:** The Choir's conductor; his baton is a shard of Ilyssa's staff.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Chord Crystals:** three colored crystals (Red/Blue/Green) orbit him and grant shields. A chord sequence is shown; hitting the crystals in the right order (red → blue → green) shatters the shield and stuns the boss for 3 s.
  2. **Dissonance:** every 20 s, a silence pulse affects all towers in r6 for 3 s.
- **Counterplay:** Precise targeting; Resonance Pylon (sonic breaks shields); Anchor Pillar.
- **Reward:** Conductor's Baton relic.

### 12. The Grand Prism (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** A stationary monolith that refracts all light into song. When it breaks, the Waste may finally finish its piece.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Spectrum Phases:** each phase the Prism is vulnerable to exactly one color-type damage group: Red (Fire), Orange (Physical), Yellow (Radiant), Green (Nature), Blue (Frost), Indigo (Arcane), Violet (Shadow/Void). Others deal 25%.
  2. **Reflection Beams:** the Prism fires beams that bounce along lanes; towers in the line are disabled. Players must position towers outside beam lines (shown by thin lines 3 s before firing).
- **Counterplay:** Loadout covering at least 5 damage types; position awareness.
- **Reward:** Prism Heart relic; Lyric Prismvale mastery.

## 8.9 Act VII — Arcane Dominion

### 13. Archmagus Veyron (Warlord)
- **Lore:** The Dominion's dean of Applied Violence. He loves a student who can out-cast him.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Spell Mirror:** every 20 s copies the attack of your highest-DPS tower and fires it back at that tower (damage disable 3 s).
  2. **Counter-Spell:** immune to Arcane while mirroring (not to other types).
  3. **Mark of Study:** the hero is marked; if they die, Veyron heals 5%.
- **Counterplay:** Spread DPS across towers; Anchor Pillar silences; keep the hero alive.
- **Reward:** Veyron's Mirror relic.

### 14. High Archon Thaumos (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** Kaldris Vane's last student and the only one who read his final thesis.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Arcane Lockdown:** every 40 s Thaumos silences all tower abilities/Overcharges and drains Mana for 8 s, unless a **Counterspell Circle** (a rune ring on the map) is occupied by the hero for 3 s. The circle appears with 4 s warning.
  2. **Rune Golems:** summons 2 Rune Golems per phase.
  3. **Final Equation (20% HP):** a 12 s puzzle: three runes light in sequence; hitting them with any damage in order negates the final blast. Otherwise 25 Integrity loss.
- **Counterplay:** Hero positioning; Anchor Pillar delay; diverse damage types.
- **Reward:** Archon's Signet relic; Aelara's mastery.

## 8.10 Act VIII — Hollow Court

### 15. Lich-Baron Malgrave (Warlord)
- **Lore:** A minor noble who kept his phylacteries safe by giving them to children as toys. They return them.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Mass Raise:** every 15 s raises all corpses in the map as skeletons. Corpses are cleared by Fire/Radiant.
  2. **Phylactery Lanterns:** 3 lanterns across the map; destroying them makes Malgrave vulnerable; while any lantern is alive he takes −60% damage.
- **Counterplay:** Burn corpses; target lanterns; Radiant; Hero Morrigan's Soul Lantern interactions.
- **Reward:** Baron's Phylactery relic.

### 16. The Hollow Queen Nythra (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** A mother who refused to let the dead go. She is not cruel, and that is the horror.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Soul-Link:** invulnerable while any Soul Lantern lives. Lanterns appear on **tower pads**, taking the slot (the tower there is sold with full refund). Destroying the lantern frees the pad.
  2. **Memory Fields:** zones that replay fallen enemies from earlier waves (spawn ghost versions at half HP).
  3. **Final Lullaby (20% HP):** her voice slows all towers by 40% for 10 s; Sanctuary/Anchor can reduce.
- **Counterplay:** Rapid lantern removal; reserve gold to re-place; Radiant.
- **Reward:** Nythra's Lullaby relic; Morrigan mastery.

## 8.11 Act IX — Celestial Order

### 17. Seraph Auriel the Unyielding (Warlord)
- **Lore:** A seraph who vowed never to retreat. The Order has since learned that this is a design flaw.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Divine Shield Cycle:** invulnerable for 6 s, vulnerable for 10 s; Shadow/Void damage hits through 50% of shield.
  2. **Sanctify:** marks zones that heal allies and damage towers; the Sanctuary Shrine's cleanse removes them.
  3. **Wing Slash:** line attack that disables towers for 3 s.
- **Counterplay:** Burst in the vulnerable window; Shadow and Void.
- **Reward:** Auriel's Pinion relic.

### 18. The Arbiter, Voice of Judgment (Act Boss)
- **Lore:** No one has seen its face. It does not hate the player; it simply keeps score.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Verdict:** tracks the tower school (or the hero) that has dealt the most damage in the last 30 s and grants itself +75% resist against that source for 20 s. A visible scale UI shows the "weight" of each school so the player can plan.
  2. **Appeal (Phase 2):** the player may use a Nexus Spell to "appeal" (cost 80 Mana), resetting the Verdict once.
  3. **Sentence (Phase 3):** tags one tower with "Condemned" — it will be disabled in 8 s unless a different tower of the same school is built; an incentive to diversify.
- **Counterplay:** Rotate dominant sources, use ≥ 4 schools; Appeal at key time.
- **Reward:** Scales of Verdict relic; unlocks Seraphine mastery.

## 8.12 Act X — Void Swarm

### 19. Hivemother Xyrrith (Warlord)
- **Lore:** The first Swarm queen to doubt. She is trying to save her children by ending the Fracture faster.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. **Endless Brood:** Rift Eggs spawn Voidlings every 10 s. Eggs have 3000 HP; each destroyed egg reduces spawn rate.
  2. **Devour:** Xyrrith eats eggs to heal 5% each; kill eggs before she reaches them.
  3. **Null Pulse:** all Mana regen −100% for 6 s every 30 s.
- **Counterplay:** AoE; egg priority; reserve Mana for pulses.
- **Reward:** Brood Heart relic; Vael mastery.

### 20. The Unmaker (Final Boss)
- **Lore:** The Eleventh Voice: Ending. It speaks in the Guardian's own voice.
- **Phases:**
  1. **Unraveling:** random tower pads vanish (telegraphed 3 s; towers refunded 100%). Pads re-appear elsewhere later.
  2. **Rewriting:** the path rewrites mid-battle; the lane layout shifts every 45 s; enemies take shortcuts.
  3. **Echo of Factions:** summons empowered echoes of 3 previous bosses chosen from those the player defeated most recently.
  4. **The Last Word:** a Nexus-wide duel: the Nexus Surge (spent in advance) and hero Ultimate must be used within a 15 s window or the Unmaker erases the Nexus Integrity by 50%.
- **Counterplay:** Flexible towers; reserve Surge and Ultimate for phase 4; diverse coverage for echoes.
- **Reward:** The Last Word relic; unlocks Auren Echo hero and the ending choice (Concord, Rewrite, Acceptance).

## 8.13 Boss Rush Variants

In Boss Rush mode, each boss is modified:
- Fixed map arena; shorter bar of waves.
- Boss HP × 0.6 but with the Mythic mechanic modifier (one extra ability).
- Between bosses the player chooses a Boon and spends accumulated Gold on an upgrade.

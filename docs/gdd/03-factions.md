# 4. Factions

Each faction is a campaign **Act**. Acts are designed so that the faction's strengths pressure a specific play style, and the player's unlocked towers provide counters.

**Template per faction:** Story · Visual identity · Units · Strengths · Weaknesses · Tactical behavior · Boss pair · Ally hero.

---

## 4.1 Verdant Covenant (Act I — Forest)

- **Story:** Caretakers of Verdancy. They believe the Nexus has stopped listening to Growth and that the Guardian must prove they still hear the world. They are the gentlest invaders and the first to be reconciled.
- **Visual identity:** Emerald, moss-brown, bioluminescent teal. Organic curves, spiral motifs, petals and seeds. Music: wooden flutes, handpan, low strings.
- **Units:** Thornlings, Bark Guards, Druids, Treants, Wolf-Riders, spore and pollen creatures (10 types, see [06-enemies.md](06-enemies.md)).
- **Strengths:** Regeneration, healing auras, high resistance to Nature, area denial through spores.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire, Storm. Slow elites. Healers are fragile.
- **Tactical behavior:** Waves feature a healer plus a pack; punish players who spread damage thin. Introduces *priority targeting*.
- **Bosses:** Sporemother Myra (mini), Old Gnarl (act boss).
- **Ally hero:** Thorn Mossbark.

## 4.2 Ashen Sultanate (Act II — Desert)

- **Story:** A civilization that entombed time itself to resist Entropy. Its sovereign, Khet-Ra, wants to freeze the Nexus in a perpetual noon. Many soldiers are the *remembered dead*, voluntarily preserved.
- **Visual identity:** Gold, sand, lapis blue, ash grey. Geometric glyphs, scarab and sun disc motifs, bandaged silhouettes. Music: oud, frame drums, droning chants.
- **Units:** Skirmishers, mummified legionnaires, scarab swarms, priests, sphinx guards, djinn (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Adaptive defenses (riddle shells), revival, mobility in sandstorms.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire (bandages), Radiant, anything that handles swarms.
- **Tactical behavior:** Introduces *damage-type variety* through adaptive resist; punishes mono-element builds.
- **Bosses:** Anuket-Zar the Riddlemaster, Khet-Ra the Ashen Sun.
- **Ally hero:** Nefari Sunscar.

## 4.3 Ember Clans (Act III — Volcano)

- **Story:** Clan-folk of Cinderhold, whose entire culture is a rite of will. After the Fracture the Heart-Flame stirred and the Clans heard it demand war. Warchief Grashka sees the Nexus as the final hearth to claim.
- **Visual identity:** Black obsidian, ember orange, molten gold. Jagged shapes, hammered iron, war paint, banners of smoke. Music: war drums, throat singing, brass horns.
- **Units:** Raiders, magma brutes, slagthrowers, shamans, berserkers, drake riders, imps (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Speed, burst, tower disabling through overheating, flying bombers.
- **Weaknesses:** Frost (hardens and slows magma), low armor among raiders.
- **Tactical behavior:** Fast pressure waves and a tower-disruption theme. Teaches *cleansing and anti-air*.
- **Bosses:** Warchief Grashka Cinderfang, Pyrrax the Caldera Wyrm.
- **Ally hero:** Kyra Emberhand.

## 4.4 Iron Collective (Act IV — Smelter Badlands)

- **Story:** A republic of artificers that cast off Dominion rule. They fear magic and have built tools to make a world that never has to ask permission of the Nexus. They are not evil, but they cannot accept a world without efficiency.
- **Visual identity:** Brass, copper, soot black, white steam. Gears, rivets, riveted plates, pipe silhouettes. Music: percussive industrial, brass, ticking motifs.
- **Units:** Rivet troopers, sappers, steam walkers, drones, hounds, siege crawlers, juggernauts (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Heavy armor, immunity to Nature/poison, ranged siege, repair.
- **Weaknesses:** Storm, Arcane (EMP effects), predictable pathing, outrangeable crawlers.
- **Tactical behavior:** Armor checks; introduces *armor-piercing and anti-magic-immunity*. Saboteurs teach protecting towers.
- **Bosses:** Foreman Mk-7, Leviathan Mk-IX the Great Engine.
- **Ally hero:** Brannoc Ironvein.

## 4.5 Frostbound Reach (Act V — Frozen Tundra)

- **Story:** Keepers of Stillness who believe the Nexus's churn causes suffering. They plan to freeze the Nexus into a state of perfect, painless preservation.
- **Visual identity:** Ice blue, white, deep indigo aurora. Hexagonal facets, fur, runic ice carvings, long silhouettes. Music: choir drones, bowed lyre, cold bells.
- **Units:** Raiders, shieldmaidens, snow wolves, rimecasters, behemoths, harpies, yetis, mammoths, wraiths (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Frost resistance, tower freezing, crowd control, frontal shields.
- **Weaknesses:** Fire, Storm (shields bypassed by chain), high HP elites are slow.
- **Tactical behavior:** Forces *AoE and chain damage* against shield walls, and cleansing against freezes. Blizzard weather reduces range.
- **Bosses:** Jarl Skadi Rimeheart, Wintermaw.
- **Ally hero:** Isolde Winterwake.

## 4.6 Crystalline Choir (Act VI — Crystal Wastes)

- **Story:** Remnants of a civilization that was one living song. After Ilyssa's silence, the shards of the Choir seek the missing stanza and believe the Nexus holds it.
- **Visual identity:** Prismatic spectrum on white-glass; cut facets, refractions, ringing tones. Music: glass harmonica, choirs, resonant bowls; each enemy has a pitched sound.
- **Units:** Sprites, prism knights, geode crawlers, cantors, colossi, mirror dancers, wyrmlets, sirens, thieves, fractal echoes (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Reflection, armor, silence effects, splitting.
- **Weaknesses:** Sonic/Resonance, Physical (shatter), Brittle.
- **Tactical behavior:** Introduces *beam reflection and positioning* and **sound-based tower silences**.
- **Bosses:** Cantor Prime, The Grand Prism.
- **Ally hero:** Lyric Prismvale.

## 4.7 Arcane Dominion (Act VII — Ancient Ruins: Academy)

- **Story:** Founded by Kaldris Vane, the Dominion treats reality as a codebase to be optimized. They now try to rewrite the Nexus using a final spell, convinced they can improve it.
- **Visual identity:** Violet, midnight blue, silver; circular sigils, floating tomes, glowing runes. Music: pipe organ, harp arpeggios, whispered Latinate chants.
- **Units:** Spellblades, constructs, spell thieves, magisters, familiars, rune golems, blink mages, phalanxes, tome wraiths, inquisitors (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Spell resistance, dispels, teleportation, mana drain.
- **Weaknesses:** Physical damage, Void, silence, mundane crowd control.
- **Tactical behavior:** Counter-buffs: tests players dependent on timed buffs and mana. Introduces *resource denial*.
- **Bosses:** Archmagus Veyron, High Archon Thaumos.
- **Ally hero:** Aelara Voss (starter; her Dominion past is revealed here).

## 4.8 Hollow Court (Act VIII — Ancient Ruins: Necropolis)

- **Story:** A court of the dead ruled by Queen Nythra. They do not hate the living; they want the Nexus to guarantee that no one is ever forgotten, by binding all souls permanently.
- **Visual identity:** Charcoal, bone white, sickly green soul-light. Hooded silhouettes, lanterns, ornate funerary stone. Music: solo cello, tolling bells, whispered choir.
- **Units:** Footmen, banshees, ghouls, bone collectors, soul binders, wraith knights, crows, lich acolytes, shade stalkers, gatekeepers (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Resurrection, soul-linking, stealth, resistance to Shadow/Frost.
- **Weaknesses:** Radiant, Fire; corpses can be cleared.
- **Tactical behavior:** Rewards *corpse management* (burning) and reveal/anti-stealth. Introduces tension with the Nexus's own Shadow school.
- **Bosses:** Lich-Baron Malgrave, The Hollow Queen Nythra.
- **Ally hero:** Morrigan Hollowmere.

## 4.9 Celestial Order (Act IX — Sky Islands)

- **Story:** Judges of the Empyrean who believe the Fracture proves that mortals cannot be trusted with balance. They will test, then take over, the Nexus.
- **Visual identity:** Gold, white marble, sky blue; halos, wings, angular architecture. Music: choir, trumpets, bells.
- **Units:** Zealots, seraphs, archers, cherubs, temple guardians, priests, gryphon knights, inquisitor seraphs, automatons, avatars (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Flying, divine shields, healing, buff purges.
- **Weaknesses:** Shadow, Void, tightly reliant on shield timing.
- **Tactical behavior:** Heavy flyer presence; purges debuffs. Rewards *burst damage* and *anti-air specialization*.
- **Bosses:** Seraph Auriel the Unyielding, The Arbiter.
- **Ally heroes:** Seraphine Dawnward, Zephyra Skyborne.

## 4.10 Void Swarm (Act X — Void Realm)

- **Story:** The Interstice's hive-mind awakened by the Unmaker. Individual Swarm-minds are beginning to feel doubt; some will defect. They are the Unmaker's instrument and its first victims.
- **Visual identity:** Obsidian, magenta, bruised violet, negative-space silhouettes. Asymmetry, geometry that does not close, glitch effects. Music: dissonant strings, stretched choirs, reversed samples.
- **Units:** Voidlings, stalkers, maws, drones, psion husks, hounds, larvae, carapaces, leeches, shredders (10 types).
- **Strengths:** Teleportation, adaptive armor, swarming, resource drain.
- **Weaknesses:** Radiant, Anchor effects, AoE.
- **Tactical behavior:** A **final exam**: combines mechanics from all earlier acts (adaptive, disable, drain, rewrite).
- **Bosses:** Hivemother Xyrrith, The Unmaker.
- **Ally hero:** Vael the Unbound.

## 4.11 Faction Interaction Matrix

| Faction | Strong vs player build | Weak vs player build | Synergy with another faction (mixed waves in Endless/Rift) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdant | Spread damage | Fire / Storm focus | Hollow (corpses feed fungal growth) |
| Ashen | Mono-element | Mixed damage + Radiant | Iron (sand-resistant machines) |
| Ember | Disable-vulnerable builds | Frost control | Iron (overheat + armor) |
| Iron | Physical-only | Storm + Arcane | Ember, Arcane (shields + repair) |
| Frostbound | Single-target focus | AoE + chain | Crystalline (frozen shards) |
| Crystalline | Beam-heavy builds | Sonic + Physical | Arcane (mirror + counter-spells) |
| Arcane | Mana-dependent builds | Physical + silence | Celestial (dispel + purge) |
| Hollow | Stealth-blind builds | Radiant + Fire | Verdant, Void |
| Celestial | Ground-only builds | Void + Shadow + anti-air | Arcane |
| Void | Static builds | Adaptive and anchored builds | Everything (Rift mode wildcard) |

## 4.12 Faction Reputation and Codex

Each faction has a **Codex** with 4 levels (Observed → Studied → Understood → Mastered), unlocked by defeating its enemies. Codex levels reveal resistances, then abilities, then counter suggestions, and finally lore and a unique cosmetic. Mastery of all ten Codices grants the **Wheel Keeper** title and the Nexus Core cosmetic aura.

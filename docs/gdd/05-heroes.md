# 6. Heroes

**15 heroes**, each a distinct playstyle, levelling independently of the account.

## 6.1 Hero System Rules

| Rule | Details |
|---|---|
| **Slots** | 1 hero per battle; 2nd slot unlocks at Hero Hall level 10 (the second hero shares 60% of earned XP). |
| **Control** | Click to move; auto-attack nearby enemies; 3 active skills (Q W E), 1 Ultimate (R). Skills use cooldowns; Ultimate also costs Nexus Energy fraction (50). |
| **Death** | Heroes are knocked out (not removed). Respawn at Nexus after 30 s (reduced by Sanctuary/Altar/Relics). Costs Gold to instant-revive (scaling). |
| **Hero level** | 1–30. Gains XP from battles (even when benched at 40%). Each level: +stat growth and 1 Skill Point. |
| **Heroic Mastery** | Levels 1–10 beyond 30, using Soul Essence; each level gives a cosmetic or capped sidegrade, plus access to **Heroic Trials**. |
| **Progression tree** | 3 branches × 3 tiers (≈ 27 nodes). One branch is "Signature", capping total points forces choices. At level 30 the player has 30 points: not enough to fill the whole tree (≈ 36). |
| **Respec** | Free at the Hero Hall between battles. |
| **Equipment slots** | 5: **Weapon**, **Armor**, **Trinket**, **Sigil** (modifies one skill), **Banner** (aura or utility). |
| **Gear** | Crafted/upgraded at the Forge; rarities Common → Mythic. Gear has capped stat budgets: no gear can exceed ±15% of hero baseline in any stat. |
| **Synergies** | Each hero has 2 "Bond" heroes (shared dialogue + small bonus when both are in the roster). |

**Stat baseline** (level 1): HP 400, Armor 5, Move 2.5 tiles/s, Attack 18 (varies). Values below indicate roles.

## 6.3 Hero Profiles

### 1. Aelara Voss — Archmage
- **Story:** Once an Inquisitor's apprentice, Aelara discovered the Dominion harvested Mana from villages that never consented. She stole a staff and her conscience, then ran to the Citadel.
- **Role:** Ranged AoE caster, fragile.
- **Skills:** **Q Arcane Barrage** (3 homing bolts). **W Mana Rift** (zone: towers inside −25% Overcharge cost). **E Blink** (short teleport, resets Barrage).
- **Passives:** **Spellflow** — each spell cast reduces other cooldowns by 1 s. **Archivist** — +10 max Mana.
- **Ultimate: Cataclysm Equation** — 3 s channel; a screen-wide chain of arcane detonations, ×2 vs Arcane-vulnerable.
- **Tree:** *Evoker* (damage), *Loremaster* (Mana/cooldowns), *Spellblade* (melee-range hybrid).
- **Equipment themes:** Staves, robes, tomes (Sigil modifies Barrage), Banner "Sigil of the Archive".
- **Bonds:** Orrin Quill, Lyric Prismvale.

### 2. Captain Rook Halloran — Ranger
- **Story:** A mercenary captain who sold his company's contracts to the highest bidder, until the Fracture swallowed his clients. Now he fights because the only employer left is reality.
- **Role:** Single-target DPS with economy utility.
- **Skills:** **Q Marked Shot** (long-range, applies Mark). **W Caltrops** (slow field). **E Grapple Dash** (reposition).
- **Passives:** **Sharpshooter** — +1 range. **Bounty Hunter** — +10% Gold from enemies killed by Rook.
- **Ultimate: Dead Eye Barrage** — Freezes time perception; 8 targeted shots in 4 s.
- **Tree:** *Marksman* (crit), *Trapper* (control), *Contractor* (economy).
- **Bonds:** Gorrum Stonehide, Nefari Sunscar.

### 3. Gorrum Stonehide — Guardian
- **Story:** A Stoneborn, born from a mountain's heartbeat. He hears the Nexus as a distant drum and has decided to be its shield.
- **Role:** Tank/blocker; holds chokepoints.
- **Skills:** **Q Taunt Roar** (forces nearby enemies to target Gorrum). **W Seismic Slam** (stun in radius). **E Bulwark** (damage reduction 50% for 4 s).
- **Passives:** **Granite Skin** — +armor scaling with level. **Unmoving** — immune to knockback/pull.
- **Ultimate: Mountain's Wrath** — Becomes a stone colossus for 10 s; his slam leaves rock walls blocking the path briefly.
- **Tree:** *Bastion* (survivability), *Tremor* (control), *Anvil* (damage reflection).
- **Bonds:** Captain Rook, Seraphine Dawnward.

### 4. Thorn Mossbark — Druid
- **Story:** The youngest of the Covenant's Wardens. Thorn believes the Nexus can be healed rather than seized; their loyalty to the Covenant is conflicted.
- **Role:** Hybrid shapeshifter (heal / tank / burst).
- **Skills:** **Q Entangle** (root zone). **W Shapeshift** (toggle Bear: tank; Hawk: mobile ranged). **E Regrowth** (heal Nexus/hero over time).
- **Passives:** **Rootbound** — standing still 3 s: +20% damage reduction. **Wild Instinct** — +10% move speed in Hawk form.
- **Ultimate: Avatar of the Greenwood** — Grows into a treant, pulses heals and roots every 3 s.
- **Tree:** *Wildshape*, *Warden*, *Seedcaller* (summons plants).
- **Bonds:** Zephyra Skyborne, Gorrum Stonehide.

### 5. Nefari Sunscar — Duelist
- **Story:** A Sultanate swordswoman who walked out of her own tomb-city when she realized immortality was the same as a cage.
- **Role:** Assassin; finishing and mobility.
- **Skills:** **Q Dune Dash** (blink-strike through enemies). **W Sandblade Flurry** (rapid strikes). **E Mirage** (decoy and invisibility).
- **Passives:** **Executioner's Eye** — +30% damage to enemies below 25% HP. **Desert Veil** — +15% evasion on sand tiles.
- **Ultimate: Eclipse Dance** — 3 s of untouchable multi-target strikes; each kill refunds cooldowns.
- **Tree:** *Duelist*, *Shadowrunner*, *Sandweaver* (control).
- **Bonds:** Captain Rook, Kyra Emberhand.

### 6. Kyra Emberhand — Berserker
- **Story:** An Ember Clan champion who dared to disobey the Heart-Flame's call. Her scars glow when she is angry, and she is very often angry.
- **Role:** Melee bruiser; fire damage.
- **Skills:** **Q Cinder Cleave** (arc + Burn). **W Molten Leap** (jump AoE). **E War Cry** (+25% attack speed to towers nearby 6 s).
- **Passives:** **Burning Blood** — +4% damage per burning enemy in range (max 10). **Cauterize** — survives lethal damage once per battle with 1 HP and releases a fire nova.
- **Ultimate: Wildfire Avatar** — Turns into an embodied inferno for 8 s; leaves a burning trail.
- **Tree:** *Berserker*, *Pyrebrand*, *Warcaller*.
- **Bonds:** Nefari Sunscar, Isolde Winterwake (rivalry bond: bonus if both are in roster).

### 7. Brannoc Ironvein — Siege Engineer
- **Story:** An Iron Collective master engineer who defected after learning the Great Engine would crush a populated city to reach the Nexus. He still keeps the Collective's rulebook in his pocket.
- **Role:** Builder; deploys turrets and buffs towers.
- **Skills:** **Q Deploy Sentry** (temporary turret). **W Overclock** (+30% attack speed to a tower). **E Demolition Charge** (AoE, armor shred).
- **Passives:** **Field Repair** — clears tower disables faster. **Efficient Design** — −5% tower build costs.
- **Ultimate: Mk-X Titan Frame** — Mounts a battle frame for 10 s with cannon and shield.
- **Tree:** *Constructor*, *Demolitionist*, *Tinkerer* (utility).
- **Bonds:** Aelara Voss (cross-faction rivalry), Garrick (Keeper) dialogue.

### 8. Isolde Winterwake — Cryomancer
- **Story:** A Frostbound ice-priestess who left after seeing her order freeze a child "for protection."
- **Role:** Crowd control.
- **Skills:** **Q Frost Nova** (slow and chill in radius). **W Ice Wall** (blocks lane for 3 s, enemies re-route if possible). **E Glacial Prison** (freeze a single enemy; not bosses).
- **Passives:** **Cold Heart** — immune to Frost status. **Chill Mastery** — slows last 20% longer.
- **Ultimate: Absolute Zero** — Freezes all non-boss enemies for 3 s and applies Brittle for 6 s.
- **Tree:** *Frostweaver*, *Glacier*, *Shatter*.
- **Bonds:** Kyra Emberhand (rivalry), Lyric Prismvale.

### 9. Lyric Prismvale — Resonant Bard
- **Story:** A shard-singer from the Choir who can hear the missing stanza in the Citadel's foundations.
- **Role:** Buffer/debuffer.
- **Skills:** **Q Resonant Chord** (towers in radius +15% damage 8 s). **W Dissonance** (enemies −armor and cast silence 2 s). **E Crystal Cascade** (rain of crystal AoE).
- **Passives:** **Perfect Pitch** — buffs refresh on kill. **Harmony** — each distinct school in range +2% buff strength.
- **Ultimate: Symphony of Spheres** — Nexus-wide haste and damage buff for 8 s; towers sing in time.
- **Tree:** *Conductor*, *Discordant*, *Cantor*.
- **Bonds:** Aelara Voss, Isolde Winterwake.

### 10. Orrin Quill — Chronomancer
- **Story:** A Citadel archivist who accidentally lived the last day of the Long Quiet nine times.
- **Role:** Time utility; support.
- **Skills:** **Q Rewind** (restore a tower's HP/disable state and cooldowns). **W Temporal Snare** (slow zone). **E Hastening** (grants +haste to a tower).
- **Passives:** **Borrowed Time** — 10% chance on any skill to reset its cooldown. **Foresight** — shows wave composition one wave further ahead.
- **Ultimate: Second Chance** — Rewinds the last 15 s of the battle once per battle (Gold, towers, Integrity restored; enemy positions reverted).
- **Tree:** *Chronarch*, *Archivist*, *Paradox*.
- **Bonds:** Aelara Voss, Vael the Unbound.

### 11. Morrigan Hollowmere — Necromancer
- **Story:** Queen Nythra's former handmaiden who disagreed about the dead's right to rest.
- **Role:** Summoner.
- **Skills:** **Q Raise Thrall** (summon undead blocker from a corpse). **W Soul Lantern** (placed; slows and drains enemies nearby, generates Soul Essence). **E Grave Chill** (cone of Frost/Shadow).
- **Passives:** **Death's Tithe** — +10% Soul Essence at end of battle. **Spectral Pact** — thralls inherit 20% of Morrigan's armor.
- **Ultimate: Legion of the Fallen** — Raises up to 12 spectral thralls from all recent corpses.
- **Tree:** *Reaper*, *Bonewright*, *Binder*.
- **Bonds:** Seraphine Dawnward (reconciliation bond), Vael the Unbound.

### 12. Seraphine Dawnward — Paladin
- **Story:** A Celestial paladin who questioned the Arbiter's judgment and was struck from the Order's roll.
- **Role:** Healer/shield.
- **Skills:** **Q Smite** (Radiant bolt; bonus vs Shadow). **W Aegis Dome** (barrier). **E Consecrate** (ground: heals allies, damages enemies).
- **Passives:** **Oath of Light** — nearby towers resist disables 25%. **Halo** — heals Integrity 1 per wave.
- **Ultimate: Heaven's Judgment** — Beam of light, heavy damage and cleanses all towers.
- **Tree:** *Templar*, *Priest*, *Oathkeeper*.
- **Bonds:** Gorrum Stonehide, Morrigan Hollowmere.

### 13. Zephyra Skyborne — Stormrider
- **Story:** A sky-island courier who learned to ride lightning because the Order's bridges kept breaking.
- **Role:** Anti-air mobility; fast response.
- **Skills:** **Q Lightning Dive** (dash, strikes air). **W Updraft** (lifts enemies, brief ground-stun). **E Static Field** (zone: slows and chains).
- **Passives:** **Windborne** — +20% speed; ignores terrain. **Stormheart** — +15% damage to fliers.
- **Ultimate: Tempest Sovereign** — Summons a map-wide storm striking randomly for 8 s.
- **Tree:** *Skyrunner*, *Thunderhand*, *Galeguard*.
- **Bonds:** Thorn Mossbark, Seraphine Dawnward.

### 14. Vael the Unbound — Voidwalker
- **Story:** The first Swarm drone to name itself. Vael walks between thoughts and rifts.
- **Role:** Gravity controller.
- **Skills:** **Q Rift Step** (teleports along path). **W Gravity Well** (pulls enemies). **E Phase Cloak** (untargetable for 2 s).
- **Passives:** **Between Worlds** — 10% evade. **Entropic Touch** — attacks reduce enemy Healing by 30%.
- **Ultimate: Collapse** — A massive implosion at target point that executes below 20% HP (non-boss).
- **Tree:** *Rift*, *Entropy*, *Seer*.
- **Bonds:** Orrin Quill, Morrigan Hollowmere.

### 15. Auren Echo — Nexus Avatar
- **Story:** The imprint of the First Guardian in the Nexus. Unlocked after the campaign ending. Auren Echo cannot be sure whether she is a person or a memory; she chooses to behave as both.
- **Role:** Adaptive hybrid; changes kit each wave.
- **Skills:** **Axiom Shift** — At the start of each wave choose 1 of 3 offered Axioms; skills Q/W/E change to that Axiom's kit (e.g., Growth: heals/roots; Entropy: DoT/drain; Will: burst).
- **Passives:** **Echo of Ten** — 3% bonus to each school the player has used this battle. **Weaver's Hand** — towers within range gain a small buff for each school present.
- **Ultimate: Weave Reality** — Rewrites the next 8 s: all towers gain +25% damage and a random extra effect; enemies randomly weakened.
- **Tree:** *Axiom Seeker*, *Loom-Warden*, *Echo of Ending*.
- **Bonds:** All heroes (small bonus when 5+ roster members are unlocked).

## 6.4 Equipment Slots in Detail

| Slot | Effect category | Example items |
|---|---|---|
| **Weapon** | Attack damage, type conversion, on-hit effects | Voss's Spiral Staff, Rook's Long Contract |
| **Armor** | Survivability, resist types | Stonehide Plate, Glacial Mantle |
| **Trinket** | Passive utility (Mana, Gold, cooldown) | Pocket Hourglass, Cartulary's Locket |
| **Sigil** | Modifies one skill (e.g., adds chain, AoE, extra projectile) | Sigil of Echoes, Sigil of Cinders |
| **Banner** | Aura for towers or allies | Banner of the Wheel, Brass Standard |

**Rarity:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic. Mythic gear grants a unique, narrative-linked effect and is obtained from bosses on Mythic difficulty or from the **Forge Contract** system (crafting with boss materials).

## 6.5 Hero Balance Guidelines

- A hero is comparable to 2–3 towers in power at equal investment; they are *never* required to win on Normal.
- Heroes cannot solo-clear a map; towers always provide the baseline defense.
- Hero death cannot lose the battle outright, only remove their contribution.
- Hero-focused boss rule: at least 2 bosses (Jarl Skadi, Archmagus Veyron) specifically punish hero-carry builds.
